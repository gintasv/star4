In a room this open, the staircase is not a way to get upstairs — it is the
first thing you see from three directions at once. The panelled feature wall
runs the full two storeys behind it, so anything fussy on the railing would
have fought with the grid of the panelling.

The answer was to keep the ironwork plain and let the two elements sit
together: slim black balusters at a consistent rhythm, a white oak handrail and
newel to tie into the treads, and white risers to pick up the panel colour.
The floor is wide-plank white oak, and the treads were matched to it so the
stair reads as part of the floor rather than an object dropped onto it.
